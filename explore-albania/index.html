About Albania

Albania is a small country in the Balkans, but it has a lot to offer. In one trip you can relax on beaches by the Adriatic and Ionian Sea, hike in the Albanian Alps, and visit historic towns that are protected by UNESCO. Albanian people are known for their hospitality, the food is fresh and tasty, and travelling is still quite affordable compared to other European countries. This makes Albania a great choice for students and anyone who wants to discover something new.
